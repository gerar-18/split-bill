# Product Vision

## Problem

Splitting a restaurant bill is tedious when products are shared, taxes and tips must be allocated, and the receipt is difficult to parse manually.

## Vision

Make bill splitting feel like a short mobile workflow:

**Scan → Review → Assign → Split → Share**

## MVP

The first product version is manual and local-first. It must support receipts, items, people, item assignment, shared items, tax, tip, rounding, and a transparent per-person summary.

## Future

- Camera capture
- On-device OCR
- Deterministic receipt parsing
- LLM-assisted structured extraction
- Cloud synchronization
- Shared receipts
- QR/deep-link collaboration
